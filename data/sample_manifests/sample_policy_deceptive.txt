Super Bright LED Flashlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

We value user trust and privacy. Super Bright Flashlight is designed to turn your mobile LED flash into a powerful torch.

Data Collection:
We only collect non-personal analytics such as device model, screen resolution, and crash logs to improve app performance.
We do NOT collect, access, or store your SMS text messages, your microphone audio, your contacts book, or your precise GPS location.
Your personal data remains strictly on your device. We never sell your personal data to any third parties.

Advertisements:
Third-party ad networks may serve generic banner advertisements inside the app.
