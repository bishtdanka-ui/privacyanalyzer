PRIVACY POLICY FOR SYNTHETIC PULSE CHAT (DEMO DATA — NOT REAL USER DATA)
Last Updated: November 2026

1. PERSONAL INFORMATION & CONTACTS
Pulse Chat accesses your device contacts list with your explicit permission to identify contacts who are also using Pulse Chat. Contact phone numbers are hashed using SHA-256 before transmission and are never stored as plain text on our servers. We do not share your address book with third-party advertisers.

2. CAMERA AND AUDIO RECORDINGS
We request access to your device camera and microphone strictly when you initiate taking a photo, recording a video, or creating an audio voice message to send within an active chat. Camera and microphone sessions are only active during direct user interaction; no background audiovisual recording is performed.

3. DATA TRANSMISSION AND ENCRYPTION
All messages, photos, and voice notes transmitted through Pulse Chat are protected with end-to-end encryption in transit. Stored attachments are decrypted only on the recipient's authenticated device.

4. USER RIGHTS AND DATA DELETION
You can revoke camera, microphone, and contacts permissions at any time via Android system settings. You can delete your account and associated profile information instantly within the application settings.
